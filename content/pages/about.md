My name is Daniel Rosenthal. **I am not a Rabbi.** I am one Jew who studies Torah and writes down what I learn.

The teachings on this site are personal reflections. They are offered for learning and conversation. They are not halachic rulings. For questions of practice, please ask your own Rabbi.

Where I draw on a commentary or a teacher, I name the source.
